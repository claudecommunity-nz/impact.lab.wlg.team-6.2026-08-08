# Kitea — walkthrough narration

Matches `walkthrough.gif` (inline) and `web/walkthrough.mp4` (~28s). Seven
captioned frames of the running product; read at a calm pace.

1. **Kitea — Wellington, seen.** A two-way channel between communities
   and their council.
2. **One live map.** Everything Wellington is telling the council, beside
   the official agency data — filter it by lens.
3. **Reported, tracked, verified.** A report moves from received to
   resolved live; once the council verifies it, it turns official on the
   public map.
4. **The council console.** Triage, hazard context, and one tap to verify
   — acting on a report is the acknowledgment the reporter sees.
5. **One operating picture.** Council updates and live feeds together:
   rivers, roads, quakes, outages, warnings.
6. **On any phone.** Report in under a minute — no app, no login.
7. **kitea.bitn.co.nz.** A production-ready prototype from Impact Lab
   Wellington 2026.
